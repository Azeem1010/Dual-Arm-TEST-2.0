LED displays host-reported state only; STOPPED does not stop motors; demo does not change LEDs.
Four-number shortcuts require --arm arm1/arm2. Single-number shortcuts are unsupported.
demo: direct goals on --arm, or alternating arm1/arm2 when no --arm; q: quit
Protocol 5: motor ACK means packet sent; LED ACK means GPIO updated. Neither proves motion state.
HOME,arm1 | GET_HOME,arm1 | READ,arm1 | HOLD,arm1 (also arm2)
HOME writes the board preset; HOLD reads current positions then writes goals; neither enables torque.
READ returns measured positions; ROS decides arrival. BEGIN/PT/END/STOP remain unsupported.
demo uses example positions 512/520, explicitly enables torque and leaves it enabled.
Check that these goals suit the mechanism before running demo; 512 is not a calibrated home.
